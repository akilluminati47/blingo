## WEATHER & LIGHTING

### Dusk/Night
- Sky goes from orange/pink sunset to deep night `#0a0e22` with stars
- Street lamps flicker on with warm orange `#ffa040` glow
- Fog thickens in rain

### Storm (Rotten One scene)
- Heavy rain streaks falling diagonally with wind
- Dark storm clouds, frequent lightning flashes (electric blue-white `#d8e4f0`)
- Thunder follows lightning at 0.3-1.5s delay
- Puddles forming on the ground reflecting the putrid beam

### Beams (Boss spawns)
- Two Horned One: Purple pillar `#b03cff`
- Crimson One: Red pillar `#ff3030`
- Infected One: Green pillar `#3ae04a`
- Rotten One: Yellow-green pillar `#b8e03a`
- Bluga: Cold blue pillar `#2b3550`
- Grandma: Warm orange pillar `#ffa040` (wider, taller, fog-exempt)
