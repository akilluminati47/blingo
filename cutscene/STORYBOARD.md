# BLINGO — Advertisement Cutscene Storyboard

**Duration target:** 60-90 seconds  
**Style:** 3D animated, cel-shaded / clay-render aesthetic matching the game's blob-cousin art style  
**Audio:** Chip-synth cousin theme medley, layered with zombie groans, gunfire, melee cracks, rain ambience, thunder  

---

## SHOT 1 — OPENING: THE BLOCK (0:00-0:06)

**Visual:** Wide aerial pull-back over the block — a low-poly town at dusk. Bank steps, church spire silhouetted, jelly park statue, fountain pavilion. Orange street lamps flicker on. Crows scatter from a roof ridge.

**Toast Overlay (game-accurate):** `"When the horde came, every blob turned . . except six cousins."`

**Audio:** Ambient wind, distant zombie moans, crow caws.

---

**Audio:** Each cousin's theme riff layers in as they're shown, building to full ensemble.

## SHOT 3 — MELEE COMBAT MONTAGE (0:18-0:32)

**Visual:** Rapid cuts of each cousin fighting:

- **Blingo** — baseball bat swings into a green zombie's skull. Crack. The zombie staggers.
- **Blazo** — leaps from a car hood, axe chopping STRAIGHT DOWN (game-accurate katana-style overhand). Splits a purple horned guard.
- **Blizzy** — blurs past two walkers, katana drawn. Both drop. She never stopped moving.
- **Blomba** — sledgehammer swings in a wide arc. Three zombies ragdoll backward into a storefront window.
- **Bloopy** — pipe cracks a droopy zombie across the jaw. Reloads his pistol in one blurry-fast motion.
- **Blondie** — machete sweeps through a crate. Ammo cascades out. She grins, pockets it.

**Audio:** Melee whooshes, bone cracks, crunching impacts.

---

## SHOT 4 — GUNFIGHT (0:32-0:44)

**Visual:** The squad opens fire. Muzzle flashes in the darkening street.

- **Blingo** — rifle aimed from behind a car, three-round burst.
- **Blazo** — shotgun blast point-blank into a charging red brute. Double-tap.
- **Blizzy** — SMG sprays a crowd of walkers shambling out of an alley.
- **Blomba** — magnum recoil. A purple guard's horn shatters. The boss's shield flickers.
- **Bloopy** — pistol cracks a headshot on a blind zombie mid-lunge.
- **Blondie** — sniper scope glint from a rooftop. One shot, two zombies fall (lined up).

**Toast:** `"Now the immune cousins are taking the town back, block by block."`

**Audio:** Gunfire layered. SMG rattle. Shotgun boom. Magnum roar. Sniper crack.

---

## SHOT 5 — THE ROTTEN ONE SPAWNS (0:44-0:56)

**Visual:** *Weather shift.* Rain begins to fall. The sky darkens from dusk to storm-black. Lightning strobes the jelly park.

The picnic ground. Puddles forming. A beam of putrid yellow-green light (`#b8e03a`) punches down from the storm clouds onto the center of the picnic tables.

Something rises from the beam. The Rotten One — massive (2.7x blob scale), sickly yellow-green (`#77a12c`). His left chest is GAPING OPEN — white rib bars arcing over a beating heart visible in the hole. One eyeball hangs loose on a red optic stalk. NO HORNS. The rot is his crown.

His minions claw up out of the wet grass around him — green, hornless, all bearing the same rotting wounds.

**Toast:** `"THE ROTTEN ONE CRASHES THE PICNIC .ᐟ"`

**Audio:** Heavy rain. Deep thunder roll. The Rotten One's guttural roar. Heart beating sound effect.

---

## SHOT 6 — THE FAMILY FIGHTS BACK (0:56-1:06)

**Visual:** All six cousins charge the Rotten One together. Lightning flashes illuminate them mid-swing.

- Blomba's sledgehammer connects with the Rotten One's chest. The rot shield flinches.
- Blingo swings his bat into a hornless guard. It drops.
- Blizzy's katana slices through two rotting minions in one arc.
- Blazo fires the shotgun into the boss's exposed heart. Green ichor splatters.
- Bloopy and Blondie work the flanks, picking off the swarm.

**Toast:** `"BEST JELLY STOPS THE ROT .ᐟ"`

---

## SHOT 7 — BLUGA AT GRANDMA'S (1:06-1:18)

**Visual:** The Rotten One dissolves into particles. But instead of grandma's warm orange beacon, a COLD BLUE beam (`#2b3550`) ignites over the Jelly House.

A figure drops from the porch roof. Black-ops blob in a ski mask with Blizzy-blue eye slit (`#6fd8ff`). FBI stenciled on his back in goopy yellow. SMG in one hand, jelly jar in the other.

**Bluga the Bad Blob.**

He turns to face the camera. Five black-ops guards fan out behind him, glowing cold. He cracks the jelly jar seal with his thumb. Holds it up mockingly.

Then — CRUNCH. The jar shatters in his grip. Purple jelly drips through his fingers onto the porch.

**Toast:** `"BLUGA THE BAD BLOB .ᐟ"`

**Audio:** Menacing synth bass drops. Glass shatter. Jelly splatter.
